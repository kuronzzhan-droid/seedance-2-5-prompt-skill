# Six complete 30-second templates (sample anthro cast, Seedance 2.5)

## When to read this file

Read it when you need a 30-second Seedance 2.5 prompt for the realistic line, the hand-painted fantasy line, or the cel-shaded line, and want to start from a finished prompt laid out in this skill's fixed sections. The six are: a night-deck dialogue scene, a fight, a first-person everyday scene, a character reveal montage, a negotiation that turns into a fight, and a ride that demonstrates anime shot grammar. Each one runs Style → References → Characters → Scene layout → Light → Sound → Shots by the second → Ambience, followed by platform settings and review checks. The reasoning behind the wording lives in `kemono-jp-drama.md` (dialogue scenes, Japanese lines) and `combat-closeups.md` (fights, close-ups) in this folder and is not repeated here.

The reference tokens are written `@Image1`, `@Audio1`. On Jimeng (the Chinese site) the same tokens appear as `@图片1`, `@音频1`; use whatever the platform inserts when you pick an asset.

Shot count: the official 30-second example has 9 shots (3/3/4/4/4/4/3/3/2 s) [official]. Japanese TV anime averages roughly 3–4 s per shot (an estimate) [multi-source], and TV drama dialogue runs about 3–6 s per shot [multi-source]. So these templates mostly use 8 shots of 3–5 s, with the shortest reaction or close-up given 2 s.

## 2. Conventions shared by all six

Tags: [official] Volcano Ark and Xiaoyunque official docs and the official sd25-pe skill; [multi-source] two or more unofficial sources agree; [single source] one source only; [author-tested] the author's own setups and lessons learned (a single source); [test run] one Jimeng generation on 2026-10-28 (a single source); [inference] derived from the material, not tested.

- **Reference images**: every character on screen gets his own images in every clip, and the character lines are rewritten in full each time [author-tested]. Crop design sheets to single views (one head, one full-body front), remove names and annotations from the image, keep the aspect ratio within 0.4–2.5 and the resolution at or below the output resolution [official]. Upload in order of first appearance and renumber the prompt to match [official]. For the hand-painted fantasy and cel-shaded lines, first remake the references in the same art style with GPT Image; realistic references pull an animated style off course [official 2.0].
- **Voice references**: two clips at most per prompt, one speaker per shot [author-tested]. A 5–10 s clip works best [official], a clean recording of one person [multi-source]. Use only voices you recorded yourself or have a licence for [multi-source]. With no audio, replace "the voice of @AudioN" with one fixed voice description and copy it unchanged into every speaking shot [multi-source].
- **Dialogue**: 「……もう一回。」「……これは命令だ。」「承知した。」 come from the sample character setups. Every other line is an example to replace with your script. When the script gives only the intent of a line, express it with action and reaction instead of inventing a full sentence for the user [official sd25-pe]. Timing was laid out at 5–6 morae per second plus about 1 s of margin per line [inference, based on multi-source speech-rate research and community practice]. For Gerard, check which stage of his speech pattern this episode is in [author-tested].
- **Placeholders**: wherever the text says "follows @ImageN", the detail is left open here (weapon, hands, scar position, Rolf's species, and so on) and handed to the reference image [author-tested: reference images take priority over text]. The scenes are examples. With your own scene image, change only "Scene layout:" and "Light:".
- **Viewpoint**: the realistic line defaults to first person [author-tested]. Templates 1, 4, and 5 are scenes where "I" am absent or only off-screen, so they are written in third person. The hand-painted fantasy line has no fixed viewpoint and is also written in third person. These are assumptions; change them to fit the script [inference].
- **Symbols**: sound effects <>, dialogue {}, music () (none of the six uses music). Section labels are plain words followed by a colon [official].
- **Platform settings** stay out of the prompt [official]. Make a 480P sample first, then 720P once it works. On Jimeng in 2026-10 a 30 s clip cost 270 credits at 480P and 600 at 720P [author-tested]. The page defaults to sample mode, so switch by hand before the final [test run]. Before submitting, run the quick check in `kemono-jp-drama.md` section 7.

## 3. Template 1: night-deck dialogue (Kael × Seren)

Demonstrates: same-side shot/reverse shot, with the axis anchored to two fixed objects (the mainmast and the rail) and screen side and eyeline repeated in every shot [official]. Kael "gives himself away with his ears first", so his ears are written before each line [author-tested]. Shot 4 is a reaction shot with no dialogue, which turns the pause into a shot of its own [inference]. The shooting star appears in the two-shot first, then the reactions follow [official]. Shot 5 closes in to a close-up and shot 6 pulls back to a medium shot [multi-source; author-tested]. Lines are 6 / 11 / 16 / 3 morae.

```text
Style: hyper-realistic cinematic footage with realistic fur layering: the direction of the fur flow is clear, with distinct zones of long and short fur; Seren's scales catch small glints in the lamplight; ears, tails, ear fins, and fabric move naturally with the action and the night wind.
References:
@Image1 (Kael, head): use only Kael's black head fur, amber eyes, ear shape, and muzzle length.
@Image2 (Kael, shipboard outfit): use only Kael's black high-collared New Year outfit, gold bamboo-leaf pattern, red sash, and full-body proportions.
@Image3 (Seren, head): use only Seren's scale colours, blue eyes, silver-white pointed horn crown, and pink ear fins.
@Image4 (Seren, full body): use only Seren's clothing, hands, and full-body proportions.
@Image5 (night deck): use only the positions and materials of the deck, mainmast, rail, and oil lamp.
Characters: the only characters in this clip are Kael and Seren, and there is exactly one of each in frame.
Kael: black wolf anthro, adult male, a complete wolf head with a long muzzle, upright on two legs, amber eyes, canine paw-hands (four fingers and a thumb); wears a black high-collared New Year outfit with a red sash; the traits only he has: the gold bamboo-leaf pattern on his clothes and the red sash (the red scarf follows @Image2). Appearance follows @Image1 and @Image2. Kael speaks with the voice of @Audio1. His wolf canines show when he speaks.
Seren: dragon prince, adult male, silver-white and blue scales, upright on two legs, blue eyes, hand structure follows @Image4; the traits only he has: pink ear fins and a silver-white pointed horn crown. Appearance follows @Image3 and @Image4. Seren speaks with the voice of @Audio2.
Each keeps his own fur, scales, and clothing; eyes keep the colours in the reference images, with a soft catchlight.
Scene layout: the deck of a wooden ship under sail at night. Seren stands with his back against the rail, facing the deck, the starry sky above him; Kael stands one step in front of him with the mainmast behind him, an oil lamp hanging on the mast. The camera always stays on the bow side of the line between them: Kael is always on the left of frame looking right, Seren is always on the right of frame looking left.
Light: the key light is the oil lamp on the mainmast, behind Kael at frame left, warm yellow, lighting Seren's face and scales; cold blue moonlight falls from off-screen upper right and draws a thin bright rim on Kael's ear tips and shoulders.
Sound: the only sounds are ambience, action sounds, and the two characters' Japanese dialogue. Voices are clean and clear; generate the audio strictly from the written lines, spoken word for word to the end. There are four lines in the whole clip, and only one person speaks in any time range. The picture is clean cinematic footage.
Shots:
Shot 1 (0–4 s): two-shot, medium, eye level, camera at chest height, locked off; place: the deck. Seren tilts his head back at the stars, Kael watches Seren, both with mouths closed. Moving details: the lamp flame sways, the long fur on Kael's neck and Seren's ear fins tremble in the night wind. At second 3 a shooting star crosses the sky above Seren's head and is gone in an instant. Ends with: Seren's gaze resting where the star vanished.
Shot 2 (4–8 s): hard cut, over-the-shoulder close shot, same place. Kael's blurred black ear and shoulder sit in the left foreground; past them, Seren's face in three-quarter view, camera steady. Seren's ear fins sink slowly to the sides and his gaze drops from the sky to the deck; as he speaks his mouth and jaw open and close with the syllables, and he closes his mouth when he finishes. Seren (Japanese, the bright boyish male voice of @Audio2, quiet after a setback, to himself): {……もう一回。} Kael listens quietly with his mouth closed. Ends with: Seren looking up at the night sky again.
Shot 3 (8–12 s): hard cut, single close shot of Kael, chest up, three-quarter view looking frame right, camera steady. Kael's ears turn toward Seren together first, the tips flick once, then stand back upright; then, as he speaks, his muzzle and jaw open and close with the syllables, and he closes his mouth when he finishes. Kael (Japanese, the low male voice in a cold big-brother manner of @Audio1, flat, to Seren): {もう遅い。中にはいれ。} Seren, off-screen, listens quietly with his mouth closed. Ends with: Kael's gaze resting on Seren's face.
Shot 4 (12–14 s): hard cut, single close shot of Seren, three-quarter view looking frame left, camera steady, both with mouths closed. Seren keeps one hand on the rail and his feet where they are, ear fins drawn close to his head; his gaze travels once between Kael's face and the stars. In this range there is only wind and canvas. Ends with: Seren still leaning on the rail, gaze resting on Kael's face.
Shot 5 (14–21 s): hard cut, single close shot of Kael; the camera pushes in very slowly to a facial close-up and Kael speaks only after it settles, his muzzle fully in frame throughout. During the push, Kael's ears press back once and stand up again, he glances away at the oil lamp, then looks back at Seren; as he speaks his muzzle and jaw open and close with the syllables, and he closes his mouth when he finishes. Kael (Japanese, the low male voice in a cold big-brother manner of @Audio1, quiet but forceful, to Seren): {一つ見たら寝ろ。……これは命令だ。} Seren, off-screen, listens quietly with his mouth closed. Ends with: Kael's ears upright and forward, gaze resting on Seren's face.
Shot 6 (21–24 s): hard cut, two-shot, medium, back at the camera position of shot 1, locked off. At second 22 a second shooting star crosses the sky above Seren's head; Seren tilts his head back at once and his ear fins spread to the sides; Kael looks up too, both with mouths closed. Ends with: the star gone, Seren turning back to look at Kael.
Shot 7 (24–27 s): hard cut, over-the-shoulder close shot, Kael's blurred ear and shoulder in the left foreground; past them, Seren's face in three-quarter view, camera steady. Seren's ear fins are fully spread and the corners of his mouth lift; as he speaks his mouth and jaw open and close with the syllables, and he closes his mouth when he finishes. Seren (Japanese, the bright boyish male voice of @Audio2, light, to Kael): {……見えた。} Kael listens quietly with his mouth closed. Ends with: Seren smiling at Kael.
Shot 8 (27–30 s): hard cut, single close shot of Kael, three-quarter view looking frame right, locked off. Kael's ears relax open to the sides; with his mouth closed he lets out one long breath, his whiskers stirring with it, and the corners of his mouth lift slightly. Ends with: Kael standing where he is, looking at Seren, the frame holding steady for about 2 seconds.
Ambience: 0–30 s: <night wind moving the canvas> and <the low creak of the hull planks> continue to the last second.
```

Platform settings: 16:9; 30 s; 480P sample first, then 720P once it works; sound on.

Review checks:
- Is each line spoken by the right character, and do two mouths ever move at once? If so, move that line to a single close shot of the speaker and keep "listens quietly with his mouth closed" [official]. Is there an extra unwritten line at 0–1 s [test run: happened once, not yet re-tested]?
- Does Kael stay on the left and Seren on the right, and is the foreground of the over-the-shoulder shots Kael's black ear [official]? If Seren's ear fins do not move in his design, delete the fin actions and use gaze and hands instead [inference].
- Readings: 「遅い」 おそい, 「寝ろ」 ねろ, 「命令」 めいれい. Rewrite any misread word in kana [author-tested]. If the muzzle's lip sync does not match, shorten the line first; if it still fails, move the line off-screen [inference].

## 4. Template 2: hand-painted fantasy fight (Wachhund × Kern.V)

Demonstrates: hold, burst, hold. Freeze before the move, burst through very few poses, freeze again after the hit [multi-source]. The ink-line impact frame flashes once in the whole clip: Japanese broadcast guidelines ask for no more than 3 flashes per second in principle [single source, primary document], and the model may also flash on its own [inference]. Each exchange states who moves first, the distance, the path, the contact point, and the result [multi-source]. Wachhund only slashes and blocks; Kern.V only throws and thrusts [single source]. The two stand at the left and right thirds, with the depth of the colonnade filling the wide frame between them [multi-source]. Shot 6 uses the author's usual short 15° orbit [author-tested]; the arc is small enough that the camera ends on the same side [inference]. No dialogue: no voices are defined here for these two [author-tested]. With voice references, one short line can go into the reset beat of shot 5 [inference]. Line-by-line explanation is in `combat-closeups.md`.

```text
Style: hand-painted western fantasy illustration: fine ink outlines with light watercolour washes; backgrounds like the painted backgrounds of a hand-drawn animated film; warm golden slanting sunlight and light shafts, with the foreground darker, the midground brightest, and the distance a pale violet-blue. Speed is shown with speed lines drawn in fine ink.
References:
@Image1 (shepherd, head): use only Wachhund's black-masked face, amber eyes, and ear shape.
@Image2 (shepherd, full body): use only Wachhund's cream linen shirt, silver pauldron, grey-blue cloak, and full-body proportions.
@Image3 (longsword): use only the shape, length, and colour of Wachhund's longsword.
@Image4 (panther, head): use only Kern.V's gold-green slit pupils, fur colour, and ear shape.
@Image5 (panther, full body): use only Kern.V's clothing, silver crescent tattoo, bare feet, throwing knives, and full-body proportions.
@Image6 (colonnade): use only the layout, columns, and light of the stone colonnade.
Characters: the only characters in this clip are Wachhund and Kern.V, both adult male anthros, upright on two legs, with exactly one of each in frame.
Wachhund: German shepherd anthro, black-masked face, amber eyes, canine paw-hands (four fingers and a thumb); cream linen shirt, silver pauldron, grey-blue cloak; the trait only he has: the black-masked face. Appearance and build follow @Image1 and @Image2. The longsword follows @Image3; there is only this one in the whole clip, the blade straight and rigid, always the same length, always in his hands.
Kern.V: black panther anthro, gold-green slit pupils, barefoot, foot and hand structure follow @Image5; the trait only he has: a silver crescent tattoo (position follows @Image5). The look and number of the throwing knives follow @Image5, and the knives belong only to him.

Wachhund is always on the left of frame facing right; Kern.V is always on the right of frame facing left; they stand at the left and right thirds, with receding columns and light shafts between them. The camera always stays on the entrance side of the line between them.
Light: the slanting sun is off-screen at rear left, and light shafts cut down from upper left through the gaps between the columns; both stand in the brightest midground light, their fur edged with a warm gold rim.

Shots:

Both are completely still; only the hem of the grey-blue cloak, the tips of their fur, and the dust in the light shafts stir in the wind. Ends with: both still frozen, about five paces apart.
Shot 2 (4–6 s): hard cut, extreme close-up of the eyes, eye level, locked off. Kern.V's gold-green slit pupils narrow sharply once, his ears flatten back, and the fur on his neck rises; the eyes are natural animal eyes with a moist highlight. Ends with: the pupils held narrow, the frame still for about half a second.
Shot 3 (6–10 s): hard cut, medium side view, locked off. Kern.V bursts forward, three running steps from right to left, and throws the knife in his right hand along a level straight line at Wachhund's silver pauldron; Wachhund raises his longsword upright in front of his shoulder, and the knife strikes the middle of the blade. <the bright ring of a knife striking a blade> At the instant of contact the picture becomes a single frame of black ink lines on white paper, then returns at once to light colour; this is the only flash in the whole clip. Ends with: the knife knocked away onto the flagstones, Kern.V stopped three paces off with his feet planted one ahead of the other.
Shot 4 (10–13 s): hard cut in the middle of Wachhund's parry, close shot, slight handheld shake, subject sharp. Shot 5 (13–17 s): hard cut, medium shot, eye level, locked off. Both reset their stances, chests rising and falling with their breath: Wachhund lifts the sword tip to chest height, Kern.V takes one throwing knife between the fingers of each hand. Shot 6 (17–21 s): hard cut, medium side view; centred on the flagstones between them, the camera orbits slowly right by about 15 degrees and settles. First strike: Kern.V throws the knife in his left hand and Wachhund bats it away with the flat of his blade, a small spray of sparks <the metallic ring of a knife knocked aside>. Ends with: Kern.V with his feet planted one ahead of the other, weight on the back foot, the knife still in his right hand; the two about three paces apart.
Shot 7 (21–25 s): hard cut, medium side view, locked off. Both hold for about 1 second, only fur tips and the cloak hem moving; then both burst toward each other at once and meet in the middle: Wachhund's sword tip stops a fist's width from Kern.V's right shoulder, and Kern.V's knife point stops a fist's width from Wachhund's chest. <the dull thud of both landing a step at once> Ends with: both frozen at the same moment, Wachhund still on the left of frame, Kern.V still on the right.
Shot 8 (25–30 s): hard cut, wide shot, the camera pulling back slowly and rising to a slight high angle. Both withdraw their weapons at once and step back one pace: Wachhund lowers his sword tip to the flagstones, Kern.V slips the knife back between his fingers, his ears rising slowly. Both characters' fur, clothes, and cloak are whole and clean, and the dust in the light shafts settles. Ends with: the frame holding steady on a wide shot of the two looking at each other across three paces, light shafts slanting into the colonnade from upper left.
Ambience: 0–30 s: <the low moan of wind through the columns> and <dry leaves scraping across flagstones> continue to the last second.
```

## 5. Template 3: first-person everyday (Bobo looks after me)

Demonstrates: first person in the realistic line. "I" am the only human in this world, and only my hands appear [author-tested]. The one take is written in time ranges, each opening with "Without cutting, the camera keeps…", because a test run was hard-cut once at the boundary between two ranges [test run]. Bobo kneels on one knee, level with my eyes: a big mane shot from a low angle gives a strange silhouette [single source], and "kneeling on one knee, torso upright" also avoids "half-crouching" being drawn on all fours [author-tested]. The last range uses his "half-beat freeze when someone looks after him" for contrast [author-tested]. Lines are 6 / 9 morae.

```text
Style: hyper-realistic cinematic footage with realistic fur layering; ears, tail, and fabric move naturally with the action. First-person view: the camera is "my" eyes, at the eye height of a seated person, moving slightly with my head; my human hands show at the bottom edge of the frame.
References:
@Image1 (Bobo, head): use only Bobo's white mane, eyes, ear shape, and muzzle length.
@Image2 (Bobo, house robe): use only Bobo's house robe, hands, and full-body proportions.
@Image3 (small kitchen): use only the room layout and the positions and materials of the wooden table, window, hearth, and wooden door.
Characters: Bobo is the only character on screen; I am the only human in this world, and only my two hands appear in frame.
Bobo: lion anthro, adult male, white mane, upright on two legs, wearing a house robe, hand structure follows @Image2, clear and stable; the traits only he has: the white mane and the house robe. Appearance and build follow @Image1 and @Image2. Bobo speaks with the voice of @Audio1. His eyes keep the colour in @Image1, with a soft catchlight.

The whole clip is one take: the camera runs continuously from second 0 to the last second, always as my eyes, and every action happens in this room.
Light: firelight from the hearth comes from off-screen right, warm orange, drawing a bright rim on the edge of Bobo's white mane; outside the window is blue dusk, and cool light comes in through the window on the left.
Sound: the only sounds are ambience, action sounds, and Bobo's Japanese dialogue; I stay silent throughout. Voices are clean and clear; generate the audio strictly from the written lines, spoken word for word to the end. There are two lines in the whole clip. The picture is clean cinematic footage.

Ends with: Bobo standing across the table, setting the cup down in front of me, the camera level with his chest and face.

Bobo (Japanese, the voice of @Audio1, gentle, to me): {ほら、ゆっくり飲みな。} Then my left hand reaches out to him and smooths down the turned-up collar of his robe. Ends with: my left hand resting on his collar.
24–30 s: Without cutting, the camera keeps looking level at Bobo: his whole body freezes for about half a second, ears locked upright and forward, then slowly easing out to the sides; with his mouth closed he blinks once, slowly, his gaze slides away and comes back, his tail sways slowly from side to side behind him, and a low soft laugh sounds in his throat. Ends with: Bobo still on one knee at my left, looking at me, firelight flickering on the edge of his mane, the frame holding steady for about 2 seconds.
Ambience: 0–30 s: <the soft crackle of logs in the hearth> and <evening wind outside the window> continue to the last second.
```

Platform settings: 16:9; 30 s; 480P sample first, then 720P once it works; sound on.

- Was there a hard cut at the 6, 12, 18, or 24 s boundaries? If so, merge into three ranges (0–12, 12–24, 24–30), each still opening with "Without cutting, the camera keeps…" [test run; inference].

## 6. Template 4: Gerard reveal montage

Demonstrates: revealing slowly from the ground up: foot → cloak → hand → tail → ears → scar and eye → full face [author-tested]. A ground-level low camera, with an empty plate generated first as reference [author-tested]. The tightest framing is saved for the turning point, and close-ups hold for less time than wide shots [multi-source]. The bell rings first, then the ears turn [official: cause before effect]. The line is formally worded and the contrast is left to ears and tail [author-tested]. Assumption: the reveal is cut in third person and the last line is delivered to camera; when joined to a first-person film, treat the camera as "me". The line is 5 morae.

```text
Style: hyper-realistic cinematic footage with realistic fur layering: the flow of the white fur is clear, with distinct zones of long and short fur; ears, tail, and cloak move naturally with the action and the wind.
References:
@Image1 (Gerard, head): use only Gerard's white head fur, blue eyes, scar position, and muzzle length.
```
